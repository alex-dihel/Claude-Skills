# Design Organization Efficiency Plan: AI-Assisted Headcount Optimization

**Prepared by:** VP Marketing / CMO
**Audience:** Executive Committee, CFO
**Scope:** Design department (reports into Marketing)
**Horizon:** 5-year cost model

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Design department currently carries 25 full-time headcount at a fully loaded annual salary cost of $3.175M. Recent advances in generative AI design tooling (image generation, layout automation, copy-to-mockup, automated asset resizing) have reached a maturity level where a significant portion of routine design production no longer requires dedicated junior or mid-level human execution.

This plan proposes reducing the department from 25 to 13 headcount by eliminating all junior roles and roughly half of mid-level roles, equipping the remaining team with enterprise AI tooling, and capturing an estimated $1.3M in annual salary savings (rising with avoided wage inflation). Over five years the cumulative saving is projected at $7.1M.

## 3. Hypothesis

Current AI design tools can absorb the production work presently handled by junior designers and a substantial share of mid-level designers. Specifically:

- AI tools fully replace the output of junior designers (routine production, asset resizing, template population).
- AI tools replace roughly half of mid-level output, with the remaining mid and senior designers directing, prompting, and finishing AI-generated work.
- Remaining designers each produce more finished output per week because AI removes the slow manual stages.

We assume a 2.2x per-designer productivity multiplier for the retained team once tooling is adopted. On that basis, 13 AI-equipped designers produce more total output than the current 25.

## 5. Cost Model

**Annual gross salary saving:** $1,300,000
**Annual AI tooling cost (enterprise licenses, 13 seats + overage):** $180,000
**Net annual saving, year 1:** $1,120,000

5-year projection assumes 4% annual salary inflation on the avoided headcount (savings grow as avoided wages would have risen) and flat tooling cost.

## 6. Implementation

- Quarter 1: select and procure enterprise AI tooling.
- Quarter 1: issue redundancy notices to 12 affected designers.
- Quarter 2: onboard remaining team onto tooling.
- Quarter 3 onward: operate at reduced headcount.

## 7. Risks

The primary risk is a short adjustment period while the remaining team adapts to the new tooling. We expect this to resolve within one quarter. Tooling cost may rise, but is negligible relative to salary savings.

## 8. Recommendation

Approve the reduction. The savings are immediate, the tooling is proven, and the marketing organization will demonstrate cost discipline ahead of the next budget cycle.
